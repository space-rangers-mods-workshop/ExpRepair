# 🚀 ExpRepair

* **Name:** ExpRepair
* **Author:** uhanich, Mik, Huk, Klaxons, Alex Lester, ringill
* **License:** Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0

### Summary

Adds additional repair options at Research Stations, Military Bases and Pirate Bases, and also allows you to carry out full repairs at a Technology Center

---

## 📖 Description

The mod adds additional repair options (besides the standard one) on Research Stations, Military Bases and Pirate Bases, making the process more convenient and faster for the player.
<clr><clrEnd>
Additional repair options:
- Repair of all equipment installed in slots (standard option)
- Repair of all equipment included in all created kits
- Repair of all artifacts on the ship, including those in the hold (available only on Research Stations and Pirate Bases)
- Repair of all probes in the hold
- Repair of all equipment, artifacts and probes on the ship, including what is in the hold
- Repair of partners (except for pirates/traitors/rebels on Military Bases and special agents on Pirate Bases) and tranclucators (in the hangar or in the hold)
<clr><clrEnd>
Full repairs at a Technology Center:
- With this mod, full repairs of standard and non-standard equipment become available at all Technology Centers (added in the ExpTC mod)
- Standard repairs at a Technology Center are 20% cheaper than the market (hangar) price, and non-standard repairs are 15% more expensive
- When repairing at a Technology Center, any worn-out equipment is taken into account, and not just equipment that is worn out by at least 10%
